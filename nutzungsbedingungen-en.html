2. Description of the service

Abgehakt is a list app (shopping, tasks, recurring reminders and notes) for Android, iPhone/iPad and Mac. The basic features — creating and editing lists, reminders, notes, export and import — are free of charge forever and work locally on your device; you need neither an account nor a subscription for them. "Abgehakt+" unlocks the additional features in return for a recurring fee: sharing lists in real time, connecting devices via QR code, the home screen widget, and unlimited building blocks (keywords of your own that turn into several ready-made entries at once when you type them; without a membership you can create up to three). Sharing in real time, connecting via QR code and the home screen widget additionally require signing in with a Google or Apple account; building blocks work exclusively on your device and require no sign-in. Building blocks you have already created remain stored on your device and keep working even if your membership ends. How many people can jointly access shared lists depends on the plan you choose. The range of features may differ between platforms — for example, there is no home screen widget on the Mac.

Abgehakt+ applies to as many devices at the same time as your plan has seats. This limit is part of the scope you book. It exists so that a membership is not used beyond the scope booked and so that the running costs per plan remain predictable. It does not cause you any additional costs.

We will enforce this limit technically from 1 November 2026. If a device is added beyond the number of your seats after that date, the additional features will no longer be available on the device you have gone longest without using; as soon as you use that device again, it moves back in and another one gives way. Your lists remain on every device, and you can continue to use the basic features there free of charge. If you reinstall Abgehakt, the installation counts as a new device.

If you took out your membership before that date, we will only enforce the limit for you after informing you in advance on a durable medium about its characteristics and the date of the change. If this impairs your use more than insignificantly, you may end your membership free of charge within 30 days (Section 327r(2) to (4) of the German Civil Code, BGB).

If you pass on a seat from your plan to another person, that seat forms part of your membership and is tied to it. If your membership ends, the seat you passed on also ends, after a short transition period of a few days. The affected person keeps their lists and can continue to use Abgehakt free of charge; the additional features are then no longer available to them until they take out a membership of their own or receive a new seat.

We make every effort to keep the service available without interruption, but we cannot guarantee this (for example during maintenance or in the event of outages at third-party providers we use, such as Google Firebase or RevenueCat).
